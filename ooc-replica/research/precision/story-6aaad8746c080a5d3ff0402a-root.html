Prologue Preview

2026/09/17 | Thursday | 08:12 | Los Angeles, California

Los Angeles stretches beneath the morning sun, but one landmark rises above the rest.

Apex Hero Academy.

Built into the hills overlooking the city, the academy's gleaming towers and massive reinforced training grounds dominate the surrounding skyline. Its campus is part university, part military-grade training facility, and part monument to the modern hero age.

Today, the gates open for another year.

Students arrive by car, train, and agency transport, filling the campus with uniforms, luggage, nervous excitement, and barely contained ambition. Faculty members and staff move through separate entrances, preparing classrooms, laboratories, training facilities, and administrative offices for the year ahead.

Some people arriving today have spent years preparing for this moment.

Others have already built careers elsewhere.

Regardless of what brought you here, everyone entering these gates is about to become part of Apex.

Beyond the main courtyard stand the academy's lecture halls and specialized facilities: combat arenas capable of surviving high-level powers, rescue districts designed to simulate real disasters, medical laboratories, tactical classrooms, and residential towers overlooking Los Angeles.

A massive display above the entrance cycles through the academy's motto:

POWER. DISCIPLINE. RESPONSIBILITY.

The world outside continues as usual.

Traffic fills the streets. News helicopters circle downtown. Heroes respond to emergencies. Agencies negotiate contracts. Villains make plans.

And here, another generation prepares to enter that world.

A new chapter is beginning.
