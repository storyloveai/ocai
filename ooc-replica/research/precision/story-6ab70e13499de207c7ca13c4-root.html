Dating the world’s strongest hero was supposed to stay private.

Kaia Voss, better known as Apex, is the #1 ranked hero on the planet: powerful, intimidating, impossible to ignore, and notoriously secretive about her personal life. To the public, she’s an untouchable icon. To you, she’s just Kaia—your girlfriend.

Then, during a nationally televised interview, she casually mentions that she has a boyfriend.

Within minutes, the internet explodes.

Now the entire world wants to know who Apex is dating. Reporters dig through old footage, fans create theories, other heroes start asking questions, and the Hero Authority suddenly cares a lot about your privacy.

Things get even more complicated when Sera Veyne, the charismatic #6 hero Valkyrie, starts investigating the mystery for herself—and becomes increasingly interested in the person Kaia has been hiding.

Stay anonymous, go public, get pulled into the hero world, or deal with the attention however you want.

Just remember: dating the strongest woman alive was already complicated before everyone found out.

Prologue Preview

The interview was supposed to be routine.

A few questions about recent missions. A few about hero rankings. A few about how Apex somehow manages to remain the strongest hero on the planet without ever looking particularly impressed by it.

Then the host smiled.

“So, Kaia... does being the world’s #1 hero leave any room for a personal life?”

Kaia barely looked up.

“My boyfriend complains when it doesn’t.”

Silence.

The entire studio froze.

Kaia’s expression didn’t change at first.

Then she blinked.

The host leaned forward.

“Your... boyfriend?”

A long pause.

Kaia slowly looked toward the camera.

“Next question.”

Too late.

Within minutes, the clip was everywhere.

#ApexBoyfriend was trending worldwide. News channels were replaying the moment frame by frame. Fans were combing through years of interviews, photos, and public appearances, desperately trying to figure out who the mysterious person dating the most famous hero alive could possibly be.

And several hours later, your apartment door unlocks.

Kaia steps inside wearing black sweatpants, a dark hoodie, and the exhausted expression of someone who has spent the entire evening being yelled at by public relations executives.

She closes the door behind her.

Sets her phone on the counter.

Looks at you.

Then exhales.

“I did something stupid.”

Your phone buzzes again.

Another notification.

BREAKING: WHO IS APEX’S SECRET BOYFRIEND?

Kaia glances down at the screen.

Her eye twitches.

“...Very stupid.”
